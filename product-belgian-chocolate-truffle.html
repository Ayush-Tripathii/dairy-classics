About Belgian Chocolate Truffle

Artisanal Craftsmanship & Heritage Flavor Experience

Crafted with passion at our modern European-standard facility in Tashkent, Belgian Chocolate Truffle celebrates Dairy Classic's 30-year legacy of ice cream artistry. We start with 100% natural, single-farm cow's milk delivered fresh daily, gently slow-churned in small batches to create an ultra-smooth, velvety consistency that melts luxuriously on the tongue.

At the heart of Belgian Chocolate Truffle lies our signature cocoa profile — rich Belgian dark chocolate folded into creamy dairy ribbons, enveloped in a satisfyingly crisp outer shell crowned with golden roasted peanuts. Every bite offers a harmonious contrast between deep roasted cocoa notes and silky cream sweetness.

Safeguarded by our unbroken -18°C sub-zero cold chain from the churning lines in Tashkent straight to local supermarkets across Samarkand, Bukhara, and beyond, Belgian Chocolate Truffle reaches you in peak artisanal freshness.
